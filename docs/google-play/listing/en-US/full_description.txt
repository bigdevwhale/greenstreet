Your great-aunt has left you her hens, her yard and her old habit: planting something living along the streets of the district. Green Street is a cozy farming game on the real map of your own neighbourhood. Sow a bed on a lawn by the road you take every day, and walking past it waters it.

BEDS BY THE ROAD
• Sow where you walk: stand by a lawn next to a pavement or a park path and tap a seed packet.
• Walking past waters the bed. «Go for a walk» keeps it working with the phone in your pocket.
• Rain and snow water for you. A bed left alone for three days goes wild, but is never lost.
• 128 varieties with their own months, days to ripen and prices. When a sparkle shows over a bed, pick it.

FINDS ON THE WAY
Every few hundred metres something turns up, depending on the place, the month and the weather: morels after rain, rowan in the frost, driftwood by the water, a forgotten packet of seeds on a bench, a neighbour's lost mitten to take back.

THE YARD
A hen house with eggs every day, pots on the windowsill, a kitchen with 95 recipes, the notice board and the people of your yard: Semyonych the caretaker, Nina Petrovna from the ground floor, and Pryanik the ginger tom who must not be fed sausage. The yard is drawn after your real home: an old city courtyard, a block of flats or a house of your own.

NEIGHBOURS
Their places are real shops and buildings of your district: the bakery, the pharmacy, the library, the market. Each gives seeds once a day, takes a present, and asks for help. Their requests and letters tell their stories over three years.

WHAT THE GROATS ARE FOR
On Saturdays Aunt Zina's market buys your harvest. Eight household upgrades each change one rule: more beds, more eggs, frost fleece, a compost box, your own stall at the fair.

THREE YEARS
Festivals, a folk calendar for every day, a balcony greenhouse and beehives in the second year, a garden shared with the whole yard in the third.

PRIVACY
The map is downloaded once from OpenFreeMap (OpenStreetMap data) and kept on the phone. The weather gets only rounded coordinates. Where you walk stays on your phone: no accounts, no analytics, no ads. Free and open source.
