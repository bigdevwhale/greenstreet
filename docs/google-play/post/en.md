Green Street is now on Google Play 🌱

A cozy farm on the map of your own district. Your great-aunt has left you her hens, her yard and her old habit: planting something living along the streets. Sow a bed on a lawn by the road you take every day, and walking past it waters it.

• Your real neighbourhood, drawn in pixel art from OpenStreetMap
• 128 varieties, finds along the way, rain and snow water for you
• A yard with hens, a windowsill and a kitchen with 95 recipes
• 22 neighbours in the real shops of your district, with stories that last three years

Free, no ads, no accounts. Where you walk stays on your phone.

Google Play: https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=app.greenstreet
