# Content format

All content lives in `app/src/main/assets/content/` as UTF-8 TSV files: one header row, then one row per
line, columns separated by a single TAB. Text never contains a TAB or a newline. Empty cells are empty
strings. Every text column comes in Russian (`_ru`) and English (`_en`); the English is a natural
translation, not a word-for-word one. The TSVs are generated by Python modules in `tools/content_src/`
(one module may write one or several files); `python tools/build_content.py` runs them all and
`python tools/check_content.py` validates everything. Read `docs/WORLD.md` first: tone, cast, rules.

Russian text: natural, native, not calqued from English. «ё» where it belongs. Typographic quotes «»,
em dash —, ellipsis …. Neighbours always say «вы» to the player (plural verbs). No real city, street,
brand or chain names. No politics, war, religion, illness drama, death on the page, alcohol (kvass and
non-alcoholic drinks only), money worries.

Months are numbers 1–12, comma-separated (`9,10`). Empty = any month.

### Groups (may be used in `needs` instead of a key)

`anyfish anymush anyberry anyflower anyherb anyveg anyjam anytea anybread anycloth anyfind`
(the checker knows which keys belong to each; flowers = chamomile calendula cornflower nasturtium
marigold sweetpea aster cosmos tulip daffodil crocus lavender hyacinth amaryllis; herbs = dill parsley
coriander mint basil thyme valerian catnip sorrel; berries = strawberry raspberry currant gooseberry

all other edible crops; finds = park and street finds).

means any variety of it; a variety key means that variety.

## crops.tsv — varieties (economy)

`key crop place sow days tends regrow yield price art ramp unlock name_ru name_en about_ru about_en`

| column | meaning |
|--------|---------|
| key | variety key `<crop>_<word>`, e.g. `radish_breakfast` |
| crop | species key from the list |
| place | `verge`, `sill`, `balcony`, `garden` |
| sow | best months to sow (northern hemisphere), e.g. `4,5,6`; any month is allowed, this is advice and drives which seeds the market and finds offer |
| days | real days to ripen when tended (7–365; autumn-sown bulbs, winter rye and bushes are long). The game runs the garden `Content.PACE` (4) times faster, so a 25-day radish is ready in 7; `tends` and `regrow` are divided the same way |
| tends | tended days required (about 40–60 % of days) |
| regrow | 0, or days until the next harvest after picking (herbs 7–60; bushes, bulbs, perennials ~365) |
| yield | items per harvest (1–6) |
| price | groats at the fair per item (1–40) |
| art | drawing family: `bulb leafy spike flower grain tall vine round bush bulbflower herb root sprout` |
| ramp | fruit/flower colour ramp: `jam apple honey gold autumn cream lilac roofB enamel green oak ginger` |
| unlock | how the seeds first appear: `start market <neighbour id> request fest letter y2 y3` |
| name_ru/en | «Редис „Французский завтрак“» / "Radish 'French Breakfast'" |
| about_ru/en | one line for the album, ≤ 140 chars: a real, charming fact or a neighbour's opinion |

Every species has 1–3 varieties; about 110 rows in all. At least one variety of each verge species is
`start`, `market` or a neighbour's daily seed so the game is playable from day one; rarer ones are
rewards. Heirloom names are fine; invented cozy names too («Бабушкин», «Петроградский ранний»).

## goods.tsv

`key kind months where time weather price rare name_ru name_en about_ru about_en`

- kind: `yard fish find staple`
- where (fish): `river canal pond lake any`; (finds): `park wood street water lawn any`; others empty
- time: empty or `morning day evening night`; weather: empty or `rain fog frost snow sun`
- rare: 0–3 (3 = a once-a-season find). Staples have `price` = market cost.

## recipes.tsv

`key needs makes qty station unlock price name_ru name_en about_ru about_en`

- needs: `key:qty` list (crop species, varieties, goods, products, groups)
- makes: product key; qty: how many
- station: `kitchen oven cellar hive workbench` (`cellar` and `hive` exist from year 2)
- unlock: `start <neighbour id> letter fest y2 y3`
- price: fair price of one product

## people.tsv (cast)

`id home kind osm seeds likes loves dislikes look name_ru name_en role_ru role_en about_ru about_en`

home = `town` or `yard`. osm = `key=value|key=value` matched in order. seeds = crop variety keys given
daily, rotating. likes/loves/dislikes = keys or groups.

## Neighbour content — `people/<id>.tsv`, one file per character

Three sections in one file, distinguished by the first column `t`:

`t  n  gap  months  when  hearts  needs  reward  text_ru  text_en  reply_ru  reply_en  sets`

- `t=line` — a greeting/small talk line when the player visits. `when` = conditions, all must hold,
  comma-separated: `spring summer autumn winter`, `rain snow frost fog sun heat`, `morning day evening
  night`, `weekend`, `y1 y2 y3` (year of play; `y2+` means 2 and later), `h3+` (hearts at least 3),
  `fest:<festival key>`, `after:<flag>`. Empty = any time. n, gap, needs, reward, reply, sets empty.
  About 50 lines per town neighbour, 40 per courtyard resident: a mix of season, weather, time,
  heart-level and year lines. 1–2 sentences, ≤ 180 chars.
- `t=req` — a request. `n` = order in the chain (1, 2, 3…). `gap` = days after the previous request was
  fulfilled (first: days after the neighbour opens; 3–40). `months` = when it may appear. `hearts` =
  minimum hearts. `needs` = what to bring. `reward` = `coins:25,seeds:rye_old:3,recipe:bread_rye,
  item:gingerbread:1,tool:can` (any combination; tool upgrades only from mikhalych). text = the ask
  (≤ 240 chars), reply = the thanks when fulfilled (≤ 240 chars). `sets` optional flag. About 36
  requests per town neighbour spread across three years (12 per year), each a small story, together a
  bigger one. Courtyard residents: about 20 each.
- `t=note` — a note/letter/scene from the neighbour that arrives on its own (`n` order, `gap` days after
  the previous note, `months`, `hearts` minimum, `needs` = flags required, empty = none). `when` may hold
  `y2+` etc. text ≤ 600 chars, signed if it is a letter. `reward` optional (a gift item or seeds).
  About 18 notes per town neighbour over three years — their life story; courtyard residents about 30
  (they carry the main yard story). Pryanik's notes are short third-person scenes.

Flags: `sets` writes a flag `<id>.<word>` (e.g. `kuzmich.retired`). Other files may test it with
`after:<flag>` in `when` or in note `needs`. Built-in flags: `y2`, `y3`, `balcony`, `hive`, `garden`,
`km100`, `km500`, `km1000`, `km3000`.

Hearts go 0–10. Requests should rise slowly: h0–2 in the first months, h5+ in year 2, h8+ in year 3.

## festivals.tsv

`key md rule year name_ru name_en notice_ru notice_en gift ribbon`

- md: `MM-DD` for fixed dates, or empty with `rule`: `maslenitsa` (moveable), `sat_last_09` (last
  Saturday of September), `sat_last_05`, `first_snow`, `first_ice`, `solstice_s`, `solstice_w`.
- year: `1 2 3` or `any`. Each festival has distinct rows for years 1, 2 and 3 (and an `any` row for
  year 4+). notice ≤ 400 chars: what happens in the district that day. gift = `item:key:qty` or
  `seeds:variety:qty` or empty. ribbon = ribbon key or empty.

## almanac.tsv

`md text_ru text_en` — 366 rows, `01-01` … `12-31` with `02-29`. One folk weather saying or garden
almanac line per day, ≤ 160 chars. Authentic Russian folk calendar flavour (names of days like
«Авдотья-плющиха» are fine as folklore), always about weather, nature, gardening, home — not faith.

## board.tsv — the district notice board

`key months needs reward text_ru text_en` — ~150 small anonymous notices: «Ищу три кабачка для
кабачковой икры — кв. 14». reward like requests. Mix of all seasons.

## ribbons.tsv

`key kind arg name_ru name_en about_ru about_en` — about 100 achievements. kind: `harvest`
(arg = species key or `any:N`), `variety` (arg = N varieties), `album` (arg = N entries), `fish`
(arg = key or `any:N`), `find` (arg = key or `any:N`), `cook` (arg = product key or `any:N`), `km`
(arg = km), `beds` (arg = N beds at once), `wild` (arg = N wild harvests), `hearts` (arg =
`<id>:N` or `all:N`), `fair` (arg = groats in one day), `fest` (arg = festival key), `days` (arg = days
played), `season` (arg = `winter` etc: a harvest every week of a season), `request` (arg = N done).
